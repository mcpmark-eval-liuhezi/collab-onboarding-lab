# Escalation Policy

When something goes wrong, or you're blocked and not sure what to do next, escalate in the following order. Start at the top of the list and only move down a step if the previous one doesn't respond or can't help.

Escalation path: teammate -> on-call lead -> manager

## Notes

- Prefer the teammate route first: most questions are answerable by someone on your own team.
- The on-call lead is your fallback for anything time-sensitive, especially outside normal working hours.
- The manager should be involved only after the first two steps are exhausted, or for anything that has organizational impact.
